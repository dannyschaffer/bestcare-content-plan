Technical checklist

✓

Crawler access is open. robots.txt allows everything, and there are no bot rules or user-agent blocks in the Netlify config. AI crawlers and search engines can all reach the site.

✓

Pages are prerendered. AI crawlers read text and don’t run JavaScript, so the article body must be in the served HTML — it is. It also means anything an infographic conveys has to exist as text too.

✓

Compliance guardrails

Insurance is financially consequential content and Best Care is a licensed intermediary, not an insurer. Anton sees every post before it publishes.

✕

We don’t rank or compare named insurers. That means leaving about 5,200 searches a month on the table — “best car insurance South Africa” (1,900/mo), “cheap car insurance South Africa” (1,600/mo), “top 10 cheapest car insurance” (720/mo) and that whole family. Our version of that intent is the existing How to compare insurance policies guide, which teaches the comparison without doing it. Being featured in someone else’s list of brokers is fine and worth pursuing.

✕

No premium figures presented as quotes. Cost articles use dated, sourced ranges and say plainly that the only real number comes from a quote.

—

“May cover”, never “will cover”. No article promises a claims outcome or implies a policy wording we haven’t seen.

—

FSP details on every post. Best Care Insurance Brokers CC, registration 1992/100995/23, Authorised FSP 8754 — same wording every time, linked to the FAIS disclosure.

—

Named author and reviewer on every article. Still outstanding. We need one named, qualified reviewer attached to each post before it goes live — who that is needs confirming.

—

No data capture inside articles. Readers route to the existing quote and contact forms.

What to expect, and when

Search Console and GA4 are live, so we have a clean baseline from launch. For the first eight weeks the only useful Google metrics are indexation and impressions — whether the posts are in the index and surfacing for the terms we targeted. Clicks lag that by months.

The per-post ceiling in this market is modest and worth being straight about. The best-performing informational post we found in South African motor insurance draws roughly 412 visits a month; most insurer blog posts land between 21 and 63. This works by accumulation, not by any single article: 10–15 posts before the internal linking starts to compound, and 6–9 months before organic traffic is worth reporting on.

Being quoted in an AI answer moves faster than ranking first, because it doesn’t require position one — and the difficulty-13 clusters have thin coverage. We’ll track it manually: ten fixed prompts, run on the first Monday of each month against ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI answers, with the response and any cited sources logged in a sheet. Twenty minutes a month, and the cited-sources column tells us who is being quoted instead of us.
